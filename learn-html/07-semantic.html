07 — Semantic HTML

Semantic tags describe meaning, not just boxes. They help accessibility, SEO, and your future self reading the code. 

<header>...site or page header...</header>
<nav>...links...</nav>
<main>
  <article>
    <section>...related content...</section>
  </article>
  <aside>...sidebar / related notes...</aside>
</main>
<footer>...footer...</footer>

Prefer meaning over empty wrappers

Use <div> when you need a generic box for layout/CSS. Prefer semantic tags when the content has a clear role.

Quick map

header
Intro / branding / top of a page or section.
nav
Primary navigation links.
main
The unique main content of the page (one per page).
section / article
section = thematic group; article = self-contained piece.
footer
Closing info, copyright, secondary links.
