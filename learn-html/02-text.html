02 — Text & headings

Headings create hierarchy. Paragraphs hold body text. Inline tags emphasize words.

<h1>Main title (one per page)</h1>
<h2>Section title</h2>
<h3>Subsection</h3>

<p>This is a paragraph of text.</p>
<p>Use <strong>strong</strong> for important words,
and <em>em</em> for stress/emphasis.</p>

<p>Line break:<br />starts a new line.</p>
<hr />
<p>Horizontal rule above this paragraph.</p>

Rules of thumb

  • Use one <h1> per page — the main topic.
  • Don’t skip levels (h1 → h3). Go in order.
  • <strong> / <em> mean meaning, not just bold/italic styling.
