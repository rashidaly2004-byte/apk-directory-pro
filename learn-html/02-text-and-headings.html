Regular text goes in a paragraph tag, like this one. Browsers add space above and below paragraphs automatically.

Inside a paragraph you can make text bold and important with <strong>, or emphasized / italic with <em>. You can also highlight text with <mark>, show inline_code_like_this() with <code>, and add a line break
right in the middle of a paragraph using <br />.
