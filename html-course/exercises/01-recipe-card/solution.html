Garlic and chilli spaghetti

The whole point of this dish is restraint — four ingredients, no shortcuts. Do not let the garlic brown, or the whole pan turns bitter and you start again.

Ingredients

  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 4 cloves of garlic, sliced thin
  • 1 dried red chilli, crumbled
  • 60 ml olive oil
  • A handful of flat parsley
  • Salt, more than you think

Method

  1. Boil the pasta in heavily salted water until just short of done.
  2. Meanwhile, warm the oil gently with the garlic and chilli.
    • Low heat. The garlic should sizzle faintly, not fry.
    • Pull it off the heat the moment it turns pale gold.
  3. Add a ladle of the starchy pasta water to the oil and swirl until it thickens.
  4. Drain the pasta, tip it into the pan, and toss hard for a minute.
  5. Stir through the parsley and serve immediately.

Notes

Serves
2 as a main, 4 as a first course
Prep time
10 minutes
Cook time
12 minutes
Keeps
It doesn't. Eat it now.
Reheated, it's a different and sadder dish.

The pasta water is an ingredient. Throw it away and you throw away the sauce.

— Every Italian grandmother, independently
